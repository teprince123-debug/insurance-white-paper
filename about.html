About

Benefits clarity before benefits decisions.

Pravven started from a simple problem: people have benefits, but they don't always understand them well enough to make a confident decision.

Built on 8+ years inside the insurance and benefits industry, Pravven exists to translate confusing plan documents and industry news into plain language — what matters, what's changed, and what to verify before you decide.

We're not trying to replace your HR team, your broker, or professional advice. We're trying to make sure you walk into those conversations already understanding the basics.

What we're building

Start with clarity. Then make it usable.

The Pravven Brief

Industry intelligence for people who work in insurance and benefits — what materially happened, why it matters, and what to watch next. Published now.

Plan clarity

Tools that take a benefits summary and surface what's covered, what it costs, what's missing, and what to confirm before enrolling. In development.

Better questions

The point isn't a verdict. It's walking into a conversation with HR, a broker, a pharmacy or a provider already knowing what to ask.

Pravven provides general information and decision support. It is not legal, medical, tax or insurance advice, is not a licensed recommendation, and does not guarantee coverage. Always confirm details against your plan documents and with your HR team, broker, carrier or provider before you enroll or rely on coverage.
