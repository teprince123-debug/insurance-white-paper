The Benefit Doc Analyzer

Plan documents are written to be accurate, not to be understood. The Analyzer reads one and tells you what it actually means for you — what it costs, what it doesn't cover, what the document never answers, and what to confirm before you enroll.

It isn't available yet. This page shows what it will do, so you can tell us whether we're building the right thing.

What comes back

Not a summary. A decision.

Plenty of tools will shorten a document for you. The harder question is what changes your decision — and what you still don't know. That's what the Analyzer is built to surface.

01

Pravven's Read

The plain-language verdict, first. What this plan is good at, where it's weak, and who it tends to suit — before any tables.

02

What it actually costs

Premiums annualized, employer funding shown separately, and gross cost distinguished from what leaves your paycheck.

03

What's missing

The questions your document doesn't answer — flagged plainly rather than glossed over or quietly guessed at.

04

What to verify

Which providers, prescriptions and facilities to confirm, with whom, and before which deadline.

05

Confidence and evidence

Every conclusion carries a confidence level and points back to the page it came from. Low confidence is stated, not hidden.

06

Questions to ask

A short list you can take into a conversation with HR, a broker, a pharmacy or a provider — already in their language.

Where this actually stands

Honest status

The Analyzer is in active development and is not open to the public. There is no upload on this site, and Pravven is not accepting benefit documents yet — the privacy, security and legal groundwork that handling them properly requires comes first, not after.

What exists today is The Pravven Brief, published and free to read. The Analyzer follows.

Want to be early?

We're looking for a small number of people willing to try it first and tell us where it falls short — employees choosing benefits, and the HR teams and brokers who help them. Tell us which you are and we'll be in touch when there's something real to use.
